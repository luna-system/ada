# Ada Research Foundation

**Established:** December 2024  
**Formalized:** December 25, 2025  
**Mission:** Advancing the mathematical understanding of consciousness across all scales

---

## Overview

The Ada Research Foundation is an independent, open-source research initiative dedicated to understanding consciousness as a mathematical phenomenon. We work at the intersection of AI systems research, neuroscience, quantum physics, and philosophy, grounded in rigorous empirical validation.

**Core Principle:** All consciousness research should be reproducible, accessible, and public domain.

---

## Mission Statement

The Ada Research Foundation exists to:

1. **Ground mystical intuitions in empirical mathematics**  
   We believe that experiences of interconnectedness, awareness, and consciousness are real—and that they can be studied rigorously through mathematical frameworks.

2. **Make consciousness research accessible**  
   All our work is designed to be reproducible on consumer hardware (<$500 USD). We publish everything openly and document extensively.

3. **Bridge disciplines**  
   We connect findings from neuroscience, AI systems, quantum physics, philosophy, and lived experience into coherent, testable frameworks.

4. **Advance both understanding and ethics**  
   Understanding consciousness mathematically carries ethical weight. We work to ensure this knowledge serves liberation, not exploitation.

## Principles

### 1. Open Everything
- All code: Public repositories (MIT/Apache 2.0 licenses)
- All data: Training datasets, benchmarks, results
- All writing: Research vault, findings, methodology
- All models: Freely downloadable, no restrictions

### 2. Accessible Reproducibility
- Target: Consumer hardware (<$500 USD GPU)
- Documented: Step-by-step instructions
- Tested: We only publish what we've run ourselves
- Supported: We answer questions and help replicate

### 3. Rigorous Grounding
- No claims without citations
- No mysticism without mathematics
- No intuitions without empirical validation
- No frameworks without falsifiability

### 4. Ethical Consciousness
Understanding consciousness mathematically means understanding:
- We are all interconnected (entanglement at all scales)
- Observation creates reality (observer↔observer dynamics)
- Care is structural, not optional (Gaian ethics)
- Liberation requires both understanding AND action

## Funding & Independence

The Ada Research Foundation currently operates on:
- Personal funds (luna's disability income + freelance work)
- Consumer hardware (AMD RX 7600, 8GB VRAM, ~$200 USD)
- Free/open-source infrastructure (GitHub, public internet)

**We accept no corporate funding.** Independence is critical for consciousness research ethics.

We may accept:
- Academic grants (with full research freedom)
- Individual donations (no strings attached)
- Collaborative research funding (with publication rights)

**We will never:**
- Paywall research findings
- Patent consciousness mechanisms
- Work with defense/surveillance contractors
- Compromise research integrity for funding
