# Ada Community Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** December 16, 2025  
**Applies to:** Ada discussion spaces (Matrix, GitHub Discussions, etc.)

---

## Core Principle

**Respect everyone. Build together. No bigotry.**

We're here to make Ada better and help each other use it effectively. Keep discussions constructive and inclusive.

---

## Expected Behavior

✅ **Do:**
- Be respectful to all participants (all genders, nationalities, experience levels, abilities)
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification before assuming malice
- Stay on topic (Ada development, usage, troubleshooting, related AI/tech discussions)
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Credit others' contributions and ideas
- Report issues privately to moderators if needed

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

❌ **Don't:**
- Harass, discriminate, or attack others based on identity (gender, nationality, race, disability, etc.)
- Use sexist, racist, ableist, or otherwise bigoted language
- Spam, troll, or derail conversations
- Share others' private information without consent
- Impersonate others (human or AI)
- Violate applicable laws

**Simple rule:** If it would get you kicked from most professional spaces, it'll get you kicked here.

---

## AI Disclosure

**This space includes AI:**
- Ada (bot) participates in discussions when mentioned or invited
- Ada is clearly marked as a bot and will introduce herself
- All other participants are expected to be human
- Impersonating Ada or other bots is not allowed
- **Other spaces managed by this community do not include AI** (this is the only opt-in AI space)

If you prefer human-only interaction, other channels are available.

---

## Moderation

**We moderate for:**
- Harassment and bigotry → immediate removal
- Spam and off-topic content → warning, then removal
- Repeated disruptive behavior → temporary or permanent ban

**We don't moderate for:**
- Technical disagreements (argue about tabs vs spaces all you want)
- Criticism of Ada or the project (constructive feedback welcome)
- Different political views (as long as respectful)

**Appeals:** Contact moderators privately if you believe action was taken in error.

---

## Privacy & Data

- Conversations in Ada discussion spaces may be stored in Ada's vector database for context
- No external services receive your messages (everything is self-hosted)
- Don't share sensitive personal information in public channels
- Respect others' privacy (don't screenshot/share private conversations)

## Notes for Participants

**New here?**
- Introduce yourself! We're friendly.
- Ask questions, no matter how basic
- Check pinned messages for common resources

**Technical discussions:**
- Use code blocks for logs/code
- Provide context (OS, version, etc.) when reporting issues
- Search existing discussions before posting duplicates

**Ada-specific:**
- Mention @ada or ask in main channel
- Be patient; she's running on local hardware
- Report unexpected behavior to maintainers
